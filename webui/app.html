ESP32-S3 LoRa Console

LoRa with plain ESP32-S3 boards: the LORA32 modem firmware (flash, 2439.5 MHz) or the esp32-sdr-trx SDR firmware (RAM, 13 cm). Chrome or Edge, boards on their native USB port.
This browser has no Web Serial. Open the file in Chrome or Edge (desktop).
Connect a board to start. Boards with the esp32-sdr-trx SDR firmware can receive or transmit; boards with LORA32 in their flash work as modems.
